# SACRO-ML Reporting Discussion

**Date:** 28 October 2026  
**Duration:** 53m 51s  
**Attendees:** Hannah Gordon, Jim Smith, Simon Rogers, Jessica Ikechukwu

## Objective

Identify the minimum requirements for releasing the first version of the new SACRO-ML reporting package and agree outstanding work items.

---

## Summary

The discussion focused on narrowing the scope of the initial release to essential functionality and avoiding delays caused by future enhancements. Key topics included:

- Report structure and presentation
- Metadata/catalogue content and governance
- Use of the conversion script
- Testing requirements
- Release and review process

The team agreed that the reporting functionality is largely on the right track, with remaining work focused primarily on review, validation, testing, and presentation improvements.

---

## Decisions

### Reporting Structure

- Report results and headline findings should appear near the top of the report.
- Technical attack parameters should be moved to an appendix.
- Interpretation layers (for example red/green indicators or "good/bad" assessments) are out of scope for the first release.
